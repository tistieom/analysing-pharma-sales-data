# analysing-pharma-sales-data
The [Analysing Pharma Sales Data](https://roadmap.sh/projects/pharmaceutical-sales-data) project from roadmap.sh.

The data being analysed is the daily sales of drugs from [Pharma sales data](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/milanzdravkovic/pharma-sales-data) "Six years data (2014-2019) on sales of drugs classified in 8 ATC categories".
The following questions are answered, each with a corresponding graph:

- What are the total sales quantities for each drug category (ATC code)?
- Which individual drug brands have the highest total sales?
- Which three drugs have the highest sales in January 2015, July 2016, and September 2017?
- Which drug has sold the most often in 2017?
- Which drug category has the highest average daily sales?
- Are respiratory drugs (R03) sold more during specific months?

The `pandas`, `numpy`, `matplotlib`, and `IPython.display` modules are used.

Analysis is stored in a Jupyter Notebook file `notebook.ipynb`. Source of data is `salesdaily.csv`.
